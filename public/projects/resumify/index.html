RESUMIFY — AI-Powered Mobile Resume Builder

Lead Developer: Ahmed Raza Platform: Flutter (iOS & Android) Domain: Mobile Engineering & AI Integration

What is RESUMIFY?

RESUMIFY is a mobile application built with Flutter and Dart that transforms raw career experience into high-scoring, ATS (Applicant Tracking System) optimized resumes. It combines automated PDF typesetting with AI feedback algorithms that analyze phrasing, metric quantification, and keyword alignment against target industry roles.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Over 75% of qualified resumes get automatically filtered out by enterprise ATS systems before ever reaching a human recruiter due to improper formatting, missing keywords, or non-standard visual columns. RESUMIFY enforces standard single-column schemas and guides candidates to write action-oriented bullet points that pass automated parsers.

How Does It Work?

  • Interactive Multi-Step Form: Intuitive mobile inputs for personal details, work experience, education, projects, and skills with real-time validation.
  • ATS Keyword Scoring: Compares the user's resume draft against target job titles to identify missing hard and soft skills.
  • AI Phrasing Improvements: Generates impactful achievement bullets using the Google XYZ formula ("Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]").
  • Vector PDF Export: Compiles publication-grade, machine-readable PDF documents directly on the device with custom typography and clean margins.
