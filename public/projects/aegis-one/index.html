AEGIS-ONE — AI-Based Phishing Prevention System

Lead Developer: Ahmed Raza Institution: Air University Islamabad Domain: Cybersecurity & Applied AI/ML

What is AEGIS-ONE?

AEGIS-ONE is an enterprise AI-based phishing prevention platform engineered to defend organizations and end users against sophisticated, multi-stage phishing attacks in real time. Unlike legacy blacklist-based security tools that only identify previously registered malicious domains, AEGIS-ONE inspects zero-hour threats through behavioral machine learning and natural language analysis.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Over 90% of organizational cyber breaches originate from phishing emails and deceptive landing pages. Modern threat actors leverage AI to craft grammatically flawless spear-phishing campaigns and ephemeral domain infrastructures that evade static IP/domain reputation feeds. AEGIS-ONE solves this by inspecting raw URL lexical characteristics, email headers, visual DOM structure, and linguistic urgency signals before the user can enter credentials.

How Does AEGIS-ONE Work?

AEGIS-ONE operates as a multi-layered security pipeline:

  • URL Detection: Analyzes lexical composition, entropy, sub-domain depth, suspicious TLDs, and homoglyph/typosquatting attacks against legitimate banking and corporate identities.
  • Email Content & Header Inspection: Utilizes Natural Language Processing (NLP) models to evaluate semantic tone, urgency triggers ("Account Suspended", "Verify Now"), and SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication mismatches.
  • Machine Learning & Risk Scoring: Evaluates incoming inputs against trained supervised classification models (Random Forests, Gradient Boosting, Transformer embeddings) and assigns an actionable 0–100 threat confidence score.
  • Browser Protection & Quarantine: High-risk URLs are blocked dynamically at the browser level or placed in an isolated sandbox view to prevent unauthorized payload downloads or credential leakage.
  • Enterprise Outlook & Mail Integration: Features automated mailbox quarantine integrations that strip malicious attachments and flag suspicious inbound communications.
